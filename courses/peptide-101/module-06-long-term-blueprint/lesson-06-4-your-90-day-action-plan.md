## CORE CONTENT

### Key Point 1: The 90-Day Week-by-Week Framework

**WEEKS 1–4: Foundation**

*Protocol*: Begin at starting dose per your Legacy medical team's schedule. Inject on your designated day(s), consistently.

*Nutrition*: Establish your protein target (0.7–1g per pound of goal body weight). Set up your tracking app or log. Hit protein before anything else, every day.

*Training*: Begin or maintain resistance training at the minimum effective frequency for your track (3x/week Fat Loss, 4–5x/week Performance, graduated loading Longevity).

*Tracking*: Daily: hunger, energy, sleep (1–10). Weekly: weight, waist circumference, symptoms.

*Medical team*: Weekly check-in with your prepared log. Report all symptoms with timing and severity. Ask the questions you wrote down.

*Course*: Complete Modules 0, 1, and 2. Understand every compound in your protocol before week 4.

**WEEKS 5–8: Optimization**

*Protocol*: Titration progressing per medical team schedule. By week 5–6, most clients are at or near their primary therapeutic dose.

*Nutrition*: Recalculate caloric targets based on current weight (Fat Loss Track). Add strategic nutrient timing if on Performance Track. Begin supplementing with magnesium, omega-3, and vitamin D3 if not already.

*Training*: Progress load and intensity. Introduce progressive overload — increase weights, volume, or exercise complexity systematically.

*Tracking*: Continue daily log. Take monthly progress photos at Week 4 transition point. Note: what's improved most vs. what hasn't moved yet?

*Medical team*: Bring quantified progress data to each check-in. Request lab marker discussion: is it time to check fasting insulin, IGF-1, or CRP based on your track?

*Course*: Complete Modules 3 and 4. Confirm your program track understanding and nutrition/training integration.

**WEEKS 9–12: Consolidation and Forward Planning**

*Protocol*: Stable at therapeutic dose. Focus shifts from dose adjustment to lifestyle integration.

*Nutrition*: The eating patterns established in weeks 1–8 should feel like new habits, not effortful discipline. This is the consolidation of behavioral change alongside the biochemical change.

*Training*: Volume and intensity at your highest point of the cycle. Recovery should be noticeably better than pre-protocol.

*Tracking*: 90-day final measurements: weight, waist, hip, progress photos, full lab panel. Compare to baseline — compile your transformation data.

*Medical team*: Schedule your 90-day strategic review with protocol evolution discussion (use the framework from Lesson 6.3). Come prepared with your current state summary and next-cycle goals.

*Course*: Complete Modules 5 and 6. You now have the full knowledge framework for long-term protocol management.

### Key Point 2: The Habits That Persist After 90 Days

The compounds in your protocol do not cure underlying metabolic or structural vulnerabilities — they manage them while your lifestyle behavior builds a more durable foundation. The behaviors you establish during these 90 days are the durability mechanism that allows your results to last:

**Non-negotiable habits for every program track**:
1. **Protein priority at every meal** — this habit should outlast any protocol cycle
2. **Resistance training 3+ times per week** — the metabolic and structural benefits are irreplaceable
3. **7–9 hours of sleep consistently** — the single most important free intervention for GH, metabolism, and recovery
4. **Weekly body metrics logging** — the feedback loop that keeps you honest and your team informed
5. **Annual biomarker review** — makes you a proactive health manager, not a reactive patient

These are not burdens. They're the compound interest of health that accumulates year over year.

Most people think of health transformation as a 90-day event. The real transformation — the one that's visible in your bloodwork, your body composition, your energy, your recovery, and your relationship with your own body — is a 2–5 year project.
