## CORE CONTENT

### Key Point 1: The Two Markets for Peptides — And Why They're Not Equivalent

There are essentially two markets for peptides in the United States:

**The pharmaceutical/compounding market**: Peptides prescribed by licensed physicians and compounded by licensed US compounding pharmacies operating under FDA oversight. These facilities are 503A (patient-specific) or 503B (outsourcing facilities) registered pharmacies. They follow Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) standards, conduct third-party testing for purity and potency, and maintain sterility protocols equivalent to pharmaceutical manufacturing. This is what you receive through Legacy Performance.

**The research chemical market**: Peptides sold online under the label "for research purposes only" or "not for human use." These labels are a legal workaround — the compounds are often purchased and used for human self-administration, but the manufacturers are not held to pharmaceutical standards. Purity varies. Concentration may be inaccurate. Sterility is not guaranteed. Heavy metal contamination, bacterial endotoxins, and incorrect amino acid sequences have all been documented in independently tested research peptides.

These are not equivalent products. They may contain the same molecule name, but the quality, safety, and reliability diverge significantly.

### Key Point 2: What GMP and Third-Party Testing Actually Mean

GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) is the FDA's framework for ensuring drug manufacturing quality. For a compounding pharmacy operating under GMP standards, this means:

- Raw materials are tested for identity and purity before use
- The synthesis process is controlled and documented at every step
- Final products are tested for potency, purity, sterility, and absence of contaminants (including bacterial endotoxins that cause fever and inflammation when injected)
- Finished products are labeled with accurate concentration
- Batch records allow for traceability if an issue is identified

Third-party testing means an independent lab — with no financial stake in the result — has verified the compound's purity and concentration against the label claim. This is the gold standard.

When Legacy Performance provides peptides at wholesale cost from licensed pharmacies, this is what's behind that supply chain. You're not paying retail markup, but you're not sacrificing the quality standards that make injectable compounds safe to use.

### Key Point 3: The Real Cost of Research-Grade Peptides

Research-grade peptides are cheaper. That's the appeal. But the true cost calculation includes:

- **Potency uncertainty**: If a vial labeled "5mg tirzepatide" is actually 3mg, you're underdosing and seeing suboptimal results. If it's 8mg, you're overdosing.
- **Sterility risk**: Bacterial contamination in an injectable can cause localized infection, abscess, or systemic infection. Endotoxin contamination causes fever, chills, and immune activation.
- **No medical oversight**: Research-grade peptides are self-prescribed and self-dosed. Without a medical team monitoring your response and adjusting based on clinical criteria, you're guessing.
- **No legal protection**: Purchasing research chemicals without a prescription occupies a legal gray area that an FDA-prescribed, physician-managed protocol does not.

The people who get consistent, documented results with peptide therapy — the kind you see in clinical trials — are using pharmaceutical-grade compounds under medical supervision. That is the model Legacy Performance provides.
