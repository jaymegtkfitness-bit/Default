## CORE CONTENT

### Key Point 1: What Your DNA Reveals That Symptoms Don't

Symptoms are downstream. They're the body's best attempt to signal that something upstream has gone wrong. But the same downstream symptom can arise from multiple upstream causes — and choosing the intervention based on the symptom alone means you're guessing.

Genetic testing reveals upstream tendencies: which biological pathways are inherently more vulnerable in *your* body based on your DNA. These are not predictions of disease — they're roadmaps of where your physiology is most likely to need support.

**What the Legacy Genetic Blueprint Protocol reveals**:

**Metabolic Genetics**: Variants in genes like FTO, MC4R, and ADRB2 influence how your body regulates body weight, fat storage, and appetite. Some people have genetic variants that make GLP-1 signaling inherently less efficient — meaning they're not simply eating too much, they're fighting a genetic headwind in their metabolic signaling that tirzepatide or retatrutide directly addresses.

**GH Axis Genetics**: Variants in IGF-1 pathway genes influence how robustly your body responds to growth hormone stimulation. Some individuals with below-average IGF-1 genetic variants don't just need lifestyle support for GH restoration — they need the GHRH signal that tesamorelin provides to get their pituitary functioning at optimal output.

**Inflammatory Genetics**: Variants in genes like TNF-α, IL-6, and CRP-related loci influence how aggressively your body mounts inflammatory responses — and how efficiently it resolves them. High-inflammatory genetic profiles often manifest as the slow-recovery, chronic joint pain, and immune dysregulation that the Longevity Track addresses.

**Detoxification and Antioxidant Genetics**: Variants in GSTM1, GSTT1, and NQO1 influence glutathione synthesis and recycling efficiency. If your genetic profile shows compromised glutathione pathway function, supplementation via your Longevity Protocol isn't optional — it's compensating for a built-in biochemical limitation.

### Key Point 2: The Genetic Blueprint Protocol Process

The Legacy Genetic Blueprint Protocol follows a four-step process:

**Step 1: Full Genetic Test**
A comprehensive genome analysis — typically via saliva sample — that covers thousands of SNPs (single nucleotide polymorphisms) relevant to metabolic health, GH axis function, inflammatory pathways, detoxification, and cardiovascular risk.

**Step 2: Body Foundations Reset**
While awaiting results (testing takes 4–8 weeks), you begin an anti-inflammatory peptide protocol — specifically TB, BPC, and NPY (neuropeptide Y) — that builds a stable biological baseline. This phase identifies how your body responds to peptide therapy and resolves the most acute dysfunction before the personalized protocol begins.

**Step 3: Genetic Blueprint Report**
A detailed report mapping your genetic variants to practical protocol recommendations. This report identifies which program track is most likely to produce the greatest outcomes for *your* specific biology, and which peptides within each track will have the most meaningful impact.

**Step 4: 3-Month Personalized Protocol**
Your optimal program track is assigned based on genetic results. The difference between a genetically-informed protocol and a symptom-based guess is the difference between hitting the target and approximating its location.

### Key Point 4: How Genetics Interacts with Each Program Track

The Genetic Blueprint Protocol doesn't create a fourth option separate from the three tracks. It assigns and optimizes *within* the existing tracks:

**If genetics show strong metabolic pathway vulnerabilities**: Fat Loss Track is prioritized. The specific choice between tirzepatide and retatrutide may be informed by genetic markers related to GIP receptor sensitivity and glucagon pathway function.

**If genetics show GH axis vulnerability**: Performance Track is prioritized. Dosing cadence, IGF-1 target range, and stacking decisions may all be refined by genetic data on GH receptor sensitivity.

**If genetics show high inflammatory burden or detoxification pathway impairment**: Longevity Track is prioritized. Genetic data can specifically identify whether BPC-157 or TB-500 should be emphasized based on the primary dysfunction (local tissue repair vs. systemic inflammation).

**If genetics show multiple significant vulnerabilities**: The sequential stacking strategy — which track to address first, and when to introduce the others — is mapped out before the first injection rather than discovered through trial and error.
