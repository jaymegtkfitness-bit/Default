# Lesson 0.3: How This Course and Your Legacy Performance Program Work Together

**Module**: Module 0 — Your Wake-Up Call
**Estimated Duration**: 10 minutes
**Learning Objective**: By the end of this lesson you will know exactly how to use this course alongside your active Legacy Performance protocol — and what to prioritize at each stage.

---

## HOOK

Think of this course as the manual that came with your Legacy Performance program — except it's one you'll actually read, because it's written like a knowledgeable friend explaining things to you over coffee, not like a pharmaceutical insert that assumes you have a medical degree.

Your Legacy medical team handles the clinical side: the prescriptions, the dosing, the check-ins, the adjustments. This course handles the educational side: the *why* behind everything you're doing, so that you can make smarter decisions, ask better questions, and get more out of every week of your protocol.

---

## CORE CONTENT

### Key Point 1: This Course Is Structured to Match Your Protocol Timeline

Your Legacy Performance program is 90 days. This course is designed to track with that:

- **Before or during Week 1**: Complete Module 0 (this module) and Module 1. Understand what peptides are and how they work before your body starts responding to them.
- **Weeks 1–3**: Complete Module 2. Learn the specific peptides in your protocol in depth — what each one is doing in your body right now.
- **Weeks 3–5**: Complete Module 3. Understand the full landscape of program tracks so you can evaluate whether your current track is optimally matched to your goals.
- **Weeks 4–8**: Complete Module 4. Optimize your nutrition, training, and tracking to amplify your protocol results.
- **Weeks 8–12**: Complete Modules 5 and 6. Troubleshoot any plateaus and build your long-term strategy.

You don't need to rush through this course in a weekend. Spacing it out so the content matches what your body is experiencing will make every lesson land harder.

### Key Point 2: The Medical Team and This Course Play Different Roles

Your Legacy Performance licensed medical team does the following:
- Prescribes and oversees your peptide protocol
- Monitors your progress with weekly check-ins
- Adjusts dosing based on your response
- Provides access to wholesale-cost, pharmaceutical-grade peptides from licensed pharmacies
- Guides you if side effects or adjustment symptoms arise

This course does the following:
- Explains the science behind what your medical team is doing
- Helps you understand *why* specific peptides are included in your protocol
- Prepares you to have more productive conversations at your check-ins
- Teaches you how to track, optimize, and troubleshoot
- Gives you the knowledge to sustain your results long after the 90-day program ends

If something feels off physically — nausea, injection site reactions, unexpected symptoms — that's a conversation for your medical team, not a lesson to look up. Use this course for education. Use your team for clinical guidance.

### Key Point 3: How the Legacy Ecosystem Supports You

Your membership in Legacy Performance gives you access to a full ecosystem, not just a prescription:

- **Genetic Testing and Personalized Blueprint**: Your DNA-based protocol design that identifies root causes and matches you to the right program track
- **Personalized Nutrition Program**: Built to support and amplify your specific peptide protocol
- **Ongoing Medical Team Oversight**: Weekly check-ins with licensed providers
- **Wholesale-Cost Peptides**: Pharmaceutical-grade, from licensed pharmacies — not the gray-market research chemicals sold on the internet
- **Community and Coaching Support**: The Legacy Performance community and team are accessible when questions come up between check-ins

As you go through this course, you'll understand why each of these pieces matters. The peptide protocol is the engine — but the nutrition program, the monitoring, and the genetic foundation are what make the engine run clean.

### Key Point 4: A Note on What This Course Is Not

This course is educational material. It is not medical advice. The information here is designed to help you understand your protocol and engage more meaningfully with your Legacy medical team — not to replace that team's guidance.

Do not use this course to self-prescribe, adjust your dosing, or make clinical decisions independently. Your licensed providers are the appropriate authority on your specific protocol. This course makes you a more informed, empowered patient — not a self-treating one.
