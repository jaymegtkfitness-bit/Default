## CORE CONTENT

### Key Point 1: Peptide Therapy Restores — It Doesn't Override

The most important thing to understand about peptides is that they are not foreign chemicals forcing your body to do something unnatural. They are signaling molecules — the same type of molecule your body already produces — that restore a communication pattern your biology already knows.

Tirzepatide, for example, mimics GLP-1 and GIP — hormones your gut releases naturally after you eat. Tesamorelin mimics GHRH — the growth hormone-releasing hormone your hypothalamus already secretes. BPC-157 is a peptide sequence derived from a protein found naturally in gastric juice.

What this means practically: peptides work *with* your body's existing receptor systems. When the dose is right and the protocol is matched to your biology, the results feel like your body finally doing what it was always supposed to do — not like something artificial being forced on it.

### Key Point 2: What the Evidence Actually Shows

Let's go through the documented outcomes by category — specific peptides are covered in depth in Module 2, but here's the landscape:

**Fat Loss and Metabolic Health**: Clinical trials on tirzepatide showed average weight loss of 15–22% of body weight over 72 weeks in people with obesity, with significant improvements in insulin sensitivity, blood pressure, and triglycerides. Retatrutide trials showed even greater fat loss potential — up to 24% body weight reduction in Phase 2 data. These are not supplement-level results. These are pharmaceutical-grade outcomes.

**Body Composition and Growth Hormone**: Studies on tesamorelin in HIV-associated lipodystrophy showed significant reductions in visceral (belly) fat and improvements in IGF-1 levels. Ipamorelin has been shown to stimulate GH release in a pulsatile pattern that mimics natural secretion — without the cortisol and prolactin spikes seen with older GH secretagogues.

**Tissue Repair and Recovery**: BPC-157 research — primarily in animal models but with a strong mechanistic basis — shows accelerated healing of tendons, ligaments, muscle, and gut lining. TB-500 (thymosin beta-4) has been studied for its role in cell migration, blood vessel formation, and inflammation regulation. Anecdotally, the recovery experiences of people on these peptides are consistently and dramatically faster than baseline.

**Cellular Protection**: Glutathione is the most studied antioxidant in human physiology. IV and injectable glutathione has been shown to reduce oxidative stress markers, support liver detoxification, and enhance immune function — particularly relevant for anyone doing intensive physical training or managing chronic inflammation.

### Key Point 3: What Peptides Cannot Do

Honesty builds trust, so here it is: peptides are not a substitute for lifestyle. They are a force multiplier.

- Tirzepatide will suppress your appetite powerfully — but if you use that suppressed appetite to eat processed food instead of protein and vegetables, your results will be suboptimal.
- Tesamorelin will stimulate growth hormone release — but GH does its best work when you're sleeping 7–9 hours and doing resistance training. Short-changing either undermines the protocol.
- BPC-157 accelerates repair — but it can't repair tissue that you continue to stress without adequate recovery.

The clients at Legacy Performance who get the best results are the ones who treat their peptide protocol as the amplifier and their lifestyle as the signal being amplified. Great signal, powerful amplifier = exceptional output.
